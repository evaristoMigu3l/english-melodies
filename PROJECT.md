# Sing With Lyrics - English Learning Through Music

## Project Overview

**Sing With Lyrics** is an interactive web application designed to help students learn English pronunciation through their favorite songs. By displaying original lyrics alongside phonetic pronunciation transcripts, students can improve their English speaking skills while enjoying music.

## Core Features

### 1. Dual-Layer Lyrics Display
- **Original Lyrics**: Display the authentic song lyrics in standard English
- **Pronunciation Transcript**: Phonetic transcription positioned directly below each corresponding word
- **Visual Distinction**: Different colors for lyrics vs. pronunciation to enhance readability
- **Perfect Alignment**: Each pronunciation transcript is precisely aligned below its corresponding word

### 2. Integrated Music Player
- Embedded audio player on the same page as lyrics
- Allows students to sing along while reading both lyrics and pronunciation
- Seamless playback controls (play, pause, seek)

### 3. Learning Experience
- Students can follow along with the music in real-time
- Visual and auditory learning combined
- Practice pronunciation with their favorite songs
- Build confidence in English speaking through music
